KBO Dia Gothic project compliance summary

Copyright and intellectual property owner: Korea Baseball Organization (KBO)

This file summarizes the project's review; it is not a replacement for the
official KBO license. The current official page and License Guide Ver.2 control.

KBO distributes KBO Dia Gothic free of charge to individual and corporate
users. Commercial use and software embedding are permitted within the official
scope, and redistribution of the original font is permitted. The embedding
table covers web, mobile, desktop, and server software fonts, mobile games,
application/system fonts, and game-device UI fonts.

CI / BI use is not permitted. This includes representative uses for company,
brand, and product names; logos and marks; slogans and catchphrases; and brand
package designs. In this project, KBO Dia Gothic must not be used as the
representative branding for the game title VectorQuake, the developer name J2M,
or related brand expressions.

The font itself may not be sold. Keep the supplied distribution form: do not
edit the TTF files or redistribute a modified or adapted font. The official
license also prohibits use on illegal sites, in false or exaggerated
advertising, and in works contrary to public order or accepted morality.

KBO may use images of printed materials and advertising materials (including
online advertising) made with the font for KBO promotion; the user may ask KBO
not to do so.

This project includes the unmodified Light and Medium TTF files. The generated
TextMesh Pro SDF assets are technically separate Unity rendering assets and are
not offered as standalone font products. The official KBO license does not
expressly address TextMesh Pro SDF assets or confirm how their distribution is
treated under its modification and redistribution restrictions. Treat this as
an unresolved license interpretation and seek KBO/KBOP confirmation before
release. Until then, do not describe SDF distribution as explicitly approved;
Static versus Dynamic atlas population does not resolve that question.

No explicit end-credit or license-file bundling requirement was identified in
the reviewed official materials. This notice is retained voluntarily for
provenance and release governance.
